# Intervention Feasibility

This package summarizes two CostBench-native intervention prototypes:

- **Evidence-Dependency Refresh (EDR):** identifies evidence and intermediate results that may have become stale after an environment fact changes.
- **Action Guard:** checks whether the next proposed tool action is supported by the current state and its prerequisites.

Both are exploratory prototypes. The completed development comparisons did not demonstrate an end-to-end advantage over a simple explicit fact-update prompt. They are retained as negative and feasibility evidence, not presented as validated algorithms.

The corresponding implementations, audits and saved results are in [the plan-update research workspace](../../research/plan_commitment_pilot/RESEARCH_MAINLINE.md).
